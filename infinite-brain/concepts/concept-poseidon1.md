# Poseidon1 over Pallas Fp

The shielded stack's hash primitive is **Poseidon1** — the Poseidon permutation over the **Pallas Fp** scalar field — with parameters: state width **t = 5**, **R_F = 8** full rounds, **R_P = 46** partial rounds, S-box exponent **α = 7**.

Round constants and the MDS matrix are generated by the canonical **Grain-LFSR** method (the Poseidon paper's reproducible recipe): taps `[0, 13, 23, 38, 51, 62]`, 80-bit seed encoding `(field_type=1, sbox=0, n=255, t=5, R_F=8, R_P=46, 30 ones)`, 160 warm-up steps; 270 round constants then a Cauchy MDS `M[i][j] = 1/(xs[i]+ys[j])`.

Critical interop contract (`src/shielded/poseidon.rs:7`): the native (network-side) copy and the circuit-side (halo2) copy share **byte-identical round constants + MDS**, so off-circuit nullifier/root computations agree with in-circuit ones.
